Summary

I help communications teams adopt AI in ways that make their work stronger and keep people in charge of it. As Kent State's senior photographer and informal AI lead, I test tools on real assignments, write the guidance and verification steps, and bring colleagues along one tool at a time. Twenty years of photography and video, four at NASA Glenn, taught me how trust in an image is earned.
