About

Rami Daud

Rami Daud is a photographer and communicator who helps teams put new tools in service of the story. As Senior Photographer in University Communications and Marketing at Kent State University, he documents student life, research and university history, runs the team's digital asset workflow, and serves as its informal AI lead. Before Kent State he was a Digital Imaging Specialist at NASA Glenn Research Center, covering Artemis and Commercial Crew, and seven of his photographs ran as NASA Image of the Day. His work has earned APEX, Communicator and ADDY awards. He writes Framing the Cosmos and is completing an MS in Emerging Media and Technology.
